## What it makes possible

History turns the underlying model into something readers can understand and communities can maintain. Published-version history, diffs, and locks scoped to fields or blocks.

## Who uses it

Authors and editors use it to compose and publish change; readers receive a coherent and traceable result.

## A concrete journey

- Choose a Book field, Post block, or Zone configuration to inspect its own history scope.
- Compare field- or block-level differences between published versions.
- Inspect lock state and scope before returning to the consuming product.

## How it works

1. A product adapter defines the field or block scope.
2. Only publication creates a formal version; draft operations remain outside formal History.
3. Versions, diffs, and locks appear within Book, Post, and Zone interfaces.

## How it connects

History does not stand alone. It shares identity, context, or workflow with Book · Post · Zone · Content Structure · Editor.

## Clear boundaries

- History is an independent core capability, not part of Editor.
- Products are not forced into a universal whole-object snapshot.
- This page does not claim automatic merge, fixed lock expiry, forced takeover, or arbitrary restore.
