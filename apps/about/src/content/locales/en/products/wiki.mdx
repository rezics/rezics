## Core purpose

A wiki page is first a multilingual, attributable, revisable Post. It can describe a work, person, character, place, or concept and connect to the real object through entity IDs and subject relationships.

The product is not another content format. It enables durable shared knowledge under clear access rules, sources, and publication history.

## What is available now

1. Create wiki-kind Portable Text Posts.
1. Connect pages to works, characters, concepts, or Zone subjects.
1. Set restricted and Realm wiki access contexts.
1. Manage navigation and knowledge entry points on Realm wiki surfaces.
1. View published revisions through publication history.
1. Retain the corresponding Zone/Realm reading context on desktop and mobile.

## Why content belongs to Posts

Home filters, character tables, work timelines, and complex feature pages are publishable content, not fixed Zone fields. Zones provide navigation, subject, and filters; Posts and Block Schema carry the actual articles and structured content so they can be cited, revised, and reused across Zones.

## In development

Source citation, version discussion, Realm collaboration permissions, entity-filter blocks, and mature wiki navigation and templates remain in development. They must share Post permissions and Block Schema so wikis do not become an isolated second platform.

## Relationships and boundaries

A wiki is not a live whiteboard and does not make each keystroke formal history. A wiki Zone organizes pages without owning cited works; one knowledge Post can serve subjects across works or books.
