## What it makes possible

Library turns the underlying model into something readers can understand and communities can maintain. It gives each person a durable place to collect works and return to them over time.

## Who uses it

Individuals preserve their own reading context while communities curate, discuss, and extend shared knowledge.

## A concrete journey

- Keep a personal collection of works without separating them from shared identity and context.
