## What it makes possible

GameBook turns the underlying model into something readers can understand and communities can maintain. A branching reading manifestation formed when Book uses GameContentStructure.

## Who uses it

Readers, creators, and community editors use this surface to read, organize, and discuss concrete content.

## A concrete journey

- Readers move from Entrance through Passage and Choice to branches, merges, or Ending.
- Authors validate entry points, stable IDs, retired nodes, and a directed acyclic structure.
- Journey and JourneyStep retain the path while Progress exposes only a general summary.

## How it works

1. Create a Book, then enable GameContentStructure.
2. Configure Entrance, Passage, Choice, Ending, and layout, then pass DAG validation.
3. Write choices to JourneyStep while retired content keeps existing paths explainable.

## How it connects

GameBook does not stand alone. It shares identity, context, or workflow with Book · Content Structure · Progress · History.

## Clear boundaries

- The parent is always Book; GameContentStructure is an optional capability mode.
- There are no variables, conditions, scripts, combat, loops, or visual-novel runtime.
- Progress does not store Journey.
