## What it makes possible

Entity turns the underlying model into something readers can understand and communities can maintain. Real and fictional entities connected to Units through CreditAttribution and SubjectAttribution.

## Who uses it

Readers use it to recognize the same work; communities use it to preserve context; editors use it to prevent duplication and fragmentation.

## A concrete journey

- Entity represents real people and organizations as well as fictional characters.
- CreditAttribution expresses authors, translators, publishers, and other contribution roles.
- SubjectAttribution expresses characters, protagonists, and derivative relations.

## How it works

1. Select a stable Entity record instead of entering an identity-less name string.
2. Choose Credit or Subject, a relationship type, the Entity, and the target Unit.
3. Review the result in Book, Post, Wiki, Picture, Media, or Software.

## How it connects

Entity does not stand alone. It shares identity, context, or workflow with Work · Book · Post · History.

## Clear boundaries

- Attribution relates Entity and Unit; Tag is not a replacement.
- SubjectAttribution is not CreditAttribution.
- Source / Provenance is a later extension, not a complete first-release product.
