## Core purpose

A feed is a core REZICS discovery surface. It does not own content: following, Realms, Zones, search conditions, and content relationships produce a bounded, paginable list that leads back to its source.

## Card-list model

The first release prioritizes card lists. Every item shares publisher, language, Realm context, participation, adding to collections, and sharing protocols; books, posts, and collections then supply card content appropriate to their own information density.

This avoids maintaining an unextendable bundle of generic cards inside the feed and avoids forcing special layouts, such as carousels, into every content item. Horizontal book lists and other curated elements fit better in Zone or Dock blocks.

## What is available now

1. Load typed content items on the home page and in Realms.
1. Continue results using cursor pagination and bounded page sizes.
1. Show a post card's primary Realm and return to the correct detail context.
1. Project summaries, work data, and appropriate actions by content type.
1. Offer replies, reactions, collection, sharing, and content-governance entry points.
1. Reuse the same trusted filtering semantics for search and Zones.

## In development

Search lists, masonry layouts, more ordering strategies, personalized recommendations, and cross-language summaries are in development. Each must preserve bounded fan-out, stable cursors, and source traceability rather than use full loading or deep offset pagination.

## Relationships and boundaries

Feeds do not own content or turn recommendations into work structure. Realms, Zones, and search determine context and conditions; cards project data; the source Unit remains authoritative for interaction and detail.
