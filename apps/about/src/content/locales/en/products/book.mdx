## What it makes possible

Book turns the underlying model into something readers can understand and communities can maintain. One product surface for a book’s identity, variants, structure, content, and attribution.

## Who uses it

Readers, creators, and community editors use this surface to read, organize, and discuss concrete content.

## A concrete journey

- See main and variants under one Book identity rather than splitting every edition into unrelated works.
- Generate chapter order from ContentStructure and reuse one Post through separate occurrences.
- Manage authors, translators, publishers, characters, protagonists, and derivative relations.

## How it works

1. Create the Book identity and connect main with its variants.
2. Arrange chapter occurrences in ContentStructure to produce the reading tree.
3. At publication, Book fields and structure enter their respective History adapters.

## How it connects

Book does not stand alone. It shares identity, context, or workflow with Work · GameBook · Release · Content Structure · Entity.

## Clear boundaries

- GameBook is a Book manifestation using GameContentStructure.
- Chapter is a protocol concept, not an independent public product.
- CreditAttribution and SubjectAttribution serve contribution and subject relations respectively.
