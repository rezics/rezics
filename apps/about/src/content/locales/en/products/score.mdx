## Core purpose

Different reader communities value different things. Light-novel readers, translation readers, and a particular work's Realm may use different dimensions, or they may prefer the simplest one-time vote. REZICS therefore puts scores in Realm context rather than offering only a seemingly global, objective average.

## What is available now

1. Score a Unit in a specified Realm score context.
1. Attach one or more scores to a review.
1. Show community score overviews and personal actions on work details.
1. Filter reviews by Realm and score relationships.
1. Preserve every score's source instead of writing aggregates as work facts.

## Why Realm context matters

Differences associated with region and language are often community differences. Rather than add ever more hidden filters to one global score, REZICS lets a Realm directly own visible rules, dimensions, and discussion context.

Simple voting should remain easy. Communities that truly need finer judgment can create specialized dimensions, avoiding a scoring system so complex that nobody uses it.

## In development

Multilingual labels for custom dimensions, versioned structural specifications, event-based scores, rankings, and dynamic collections need stronger governance. The first phase prioritizes dimensions the platform can fully localize, then gradually opens Realm extensions.

## Relationships and boundaries

A score is neither a review nor source-free ranking truth. It provides structured comparison; text reviews retain reasons. Recommendations may use score signals but must not write their results back as public work data.
