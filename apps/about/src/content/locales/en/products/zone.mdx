## What it makes possible

Zone turns the underlying model into something readers can understand and communities can maintain. A community space composed from queries, configuration, and block protocols.

## Who uses it

Individuals preserve their own reading context while communities curate, discuss, and extend shared knowledge.

## A concrete journey

- Compose headers, feeds, curated lists, and other blocks through Block Schema.
- Use queries and configuration to decide what appears without transferring ownership.
- Track published configuration through a Zone-specific History adapter.

## How it works

1. Select blocks and configure each query or reference.
2. Verify feed cards and work results in the product preview.
3. Publish the configuration as a traceable Zone version.

## How it connects

Zone does not stand alone. It shares identity, context, or workflow with Realm · Feed · Collection · Tag · History.

## Clear boundaries

- Zone is a query-and-configuration space, not a content ownership container.
- Block Schema and Zone Atom remain internal protocols.
- Feed owns content streams; carousel behavior belongs to a Dock-like surface.
