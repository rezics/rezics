**Contribution** means the content that a Contributor submits, uploads, creates, designates,

**Translation Contributor** means a Contributor who creates, submits, or revises a Translation

**Monetization Terms** means the separate terms governing paid offerings that the Unit Controller

**Content Structure** means a Service resource associated with a Unit that records the selection,
grouping, hierarchy, or ordering of other content Units.

**Content-Bearing Structure** means a Content Structure that the Service expressly presents as
the Unit's hosted contents, including its chapters, episodes, tracks, or other component works.
It does not include a structure used only for navigation, taxonomy, discovery, or external
references.

**Post** means a content Unit that the Service identifies as a Post, including a Post used as a
chapter or another component of a Content-Bearing Structure.

**Translation Version** means a good-faith rendering of all or part of Licensed Content from one

for linguistic accuracy, fluency, and locally appropriate expression. It does not include a

**Published Translation Version** means a Translation Version that REZICS accepts and makes
available through the Service as a translation of the relevant content.

**Access-Qualified Translator** means a user who:

1. is authorized through the Service to access the source content to be translated;
2. uses translation functionality that the Service has enabled for that content; and
3. affirmatively accepts the terms presented for Translation Contributions before submitting one.

**Post Package** means a Post's complete hosted original content, its revisions and replacement
versions, and every Published Translation Version associated with that Post, whether the
Translation Version is published before or after a user obtains access to the Post Package.

**Post Purchase** means one completed, non-recurring transaction through which a user obtains a
paid entitlement to access a Post Package. It does not include access provided solely through a
subscription.

**Licensed Content** means:

1. all text, images, audio, audiovisual material, files, titles, descriptions, captions, cover

3. the complete hosted content of every Post, Video, Audio, or other content Unit that the

Unit, and no separate license grant is required for that content Unit.
