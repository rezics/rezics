## A public index that keeps context attached

Rezics treats every public work as something that can be cataloged, discussed, related, and revisited across languages. The about site carries that stable product story while the live app handles the interactive catalog workflows.

## Community knowledge beside the work

The goal is not a separate marketing layer. The public narrative points back to the same concepts the product uses: Unit records, realms, shelves, tags, discussions, and shared classification.
