## Product boundary

This page explains what Rezics is. Search, saving, voting, editing, joining
realms, and wiki authoring remain in the main Rezics app where authentication and
application state already live.

## Future products

The product page should be able to introduce more Rezics products later. Today,
the first product is Rezics itself: the shared work catalog and community
knowledge system.
