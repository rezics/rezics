Dependencies: selected M01 identity/access, M03 exact publication/adoption, M06
Realm membership/governance/discussion and M09 scoped discovery/delivery contracts.
Owners: [Subscribe](../../architecture/subscriptions.md),

## Activation and scope

The target is selected; runtime implementation is not activated by the documentation
task. The [plan](../README.md#active-execution) remains the sole active scope/phase
and progress owner. When activated, select dependency-ready contracts across these
modules and follow implementation, test-authoring, verification and repair phases.
Do not defer native multi-plan semantics or gift independence to a later redesign.

First runtime activation delivers the generic Subscribe core and ordinary official
Rezics Pro Realm with its complete participation/discovery behavior. Pro is the first
operated offering. Person and non-Pro Realm examples must qualify generic boundaries;
opening self-service sales for every operator is a separate rollout choice.

## Remaining deliverables and order

1. Implement offering/plan/price revisions, replaceable and parallel plan groups,
   exact beneficiary/seller/target references, multi-source grants and their native
   IAM bridge. Preserve gift-versus-purchase independence through persistence,
   commercial quotes, management APIs, caches and every existing affected consumer.
2. Implement provider-independent purchase/change/cancel intents, verified settlement,
   receipts, event dedupe, bounded reconciliation and independent awards/revocation.
   Elect/configure the initial provider, prices, collection/grace/refund/termination
   policies before dependent commercial activation. Generate APIs/SDKs together.
3. Implement generic Realm participation policy revisions, separate publication and
   review-resource meters, compensation and bounded AI jobs/human decisions. Cover
   creation, explicit additional publication, adoption, editing, republishing and
   reply paths so existing commands cannot bypass policy.
4. Complete exact accepted-version publication/adoption and scoped discussion reads.
   Keep the accepted Pro version while a new source version is reviewed. Implement
   matching Search text/snippets/media, local counts/ranking, notices and exports.
   A current-head-only search index cannot qualify this step.
5. Configure the official Pro Realm and offering through ordinary operations. Provide
   plan comparison/purchase, separate purchased/awarded/effective views, review and
   appeal states, and a Pro scope carried through shared discovery/detail surfaces.
   Preserve normal Realm/catalog behavior and the full community relationship journey.
6. Qualify SUB01-SUB24, PRO01-PRO24 and CAPSUB01-CAPSUB08 with relevant existing
   IAM/composition/governance cases. Then qualify SUBUX01-SUBUX06 at the applicable
   UI phase. Record actual G2/G3 and scoped integration evidence separately from
   implementation; provider, AI-quality, recovery and capacity gaps remain visible.

## Completion criteria

Paid subscriptions and independent grants coexist without changing each other's
commercial state. Lower-plan purchase remains possible under a higher gift.
Benefit resolution respects source time/revocation, native subject/representation
and resource authority. Multiple plans and plan groups work for both Realm and
Person offering contracts without a universal account tier.

Pro uses ordinary Realm policies and effective publication scope. Unreviewed edits,
general replies/activity and incompatible cursors cannot enter that scope. Search,
body/media and counts use the same accepted selection. Empty/sparse results never
fall back to general content. Local Pro decisions preserve other authorized uses.

Metering, provider/review retries, source compensation, privacy and restoration are
qualified under concurrency and recorded 500M/3B capacity obligations. No live sale
opens while the chosen provider/fulfillment path is unavailable or unqualified.

## Later activation

Third-party seller onboarding/payouts, corporate seats, buying transferable gifts,
arbitrary recursive bundles, cross-database operation and multi-resource commercial
partnerships require their own elected contracts. They are not needed to represent
multiple native plans or operate the first Pro Realm. Existing ordinary communities
remain available; opening wider general-community operations does not turn Pro
content public or add a global paid-author filter.
